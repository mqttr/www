Homelab

By far one of my most proud projects is my homelab. Currently running on an old Dell Optiplex in the corner of my room; it hosts a wide variety of open-source services and services written by yours truly, all containerized for easy transport. I even hosted some of my own OCI images on my self-hosted Forgejo instance for easy configuration! Now while I am quite a bit proud of a lot of these projects I got working, I am particularly proud of my Forgejo (with CI/CD workflows!), this website you see here, my configuration for my OIDC provider (Pocket-ID - which I will not link, though I will happily discuss), and my proxied Minecraft server that I host for my friends and family (also not linked).

Dotfiles

One of the things that I have been absolutely loving since moving to Linux away from Microsoft's operating system, is the motivation to write my own dotfiles. It has required and enabled me to learn a lot about better options for the various software that I use daily. It's dramatically improved my quality of life and has been insanely fun.
